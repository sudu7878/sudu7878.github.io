Shinnosuke Indian Theme Remix

0:000:00

When I listened to the Indian-remix version of the classic "Orawa Ninkimono" song, I, of course, liked it, but I felt something was missing lol. I noticed that they used Sitar for few parts. So for remixing, I reverse-engineered the notes and added it more accross the entire track. It was tedious, but was fun. Next, to make it more punchy and "dancey", I added drums and few sound fx in the beginning. This was a random sunday project and now when i listen to it, it feels cringe. But hey, this was my best shot lol.
